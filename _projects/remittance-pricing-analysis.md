## The brief

A remittance client needed to understand how its competitors really price. Headline fees are easy to read, but much of the money is made elsewhere.

## What I did

I analysed more than 20 million datapoints across 10 firms in Python. The data showed the sector moving away from fixed fees and towards **FX-margin pricing**, where the cost sits in the exchange rate rather than on the fee line.

I paired this with primary UX research, so that the findings informed both product and differentiation strategy and not only the pricing model.
